### English Version (גרסה אנגלית):
```text
Pirsumoto Remote Control is a dedicated B2B companion app built for authorized automotive dealerships, car lot managers, and sales agents in Israel.

The app provides full remote control, fast vehicle inventory intake, live task queue tracking, and real-time Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) supervision over car advertising processes on leading portals (including Yad2 and Facebook Marketplace).

Key Features:
• 📱 Smart Vehicle Intake Chatbot: Enter a license plate or record a quick voice message — the system parses vehicle specs instantly.
• 📝 Inline Data Completion: Interactive forms inside the chat to quickly update mileage, asking price, ownership history, and vehicle condition.
• 🚗 Official Vehicle Specs Lookup: Automated retrieval and validation of technical specifications, MOT test expiry, and ownership history from open government data (data.gov.il).
• 👁️ Live Screen Streaming & HITL Control: Real-time WebRTC screen monitoring with remote intervention and touch control.
• 📊 Connected Devices Dashboard: View online status of field devices, select active sessions, and switch between workflows.
• 📋 Publications Queue & Inventory: Track live advertisement statuses across classifieds, view confirmation badges, and inspect saved inventory.
• 🔒 Secure B2B Workspace: Encrypted authentication, role-based access, and enterprise data protection.

Government Data Disclaimer:
This application does not represent any government entity. Vehicle specification data is retrieved from official open government databases provided by the Israeli Ministry of Transport under the open data initiative (data.gov.il - https://data.gov.il/dataset/rechev).
```
